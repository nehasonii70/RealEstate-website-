Buyer's Acquisition Playbook

1

RERA Certification Review

Ensure the project possesses a valid RERA registration code issued by GUJRERA. DreamNest filters out all non-RERA certified builder properties.

2

Stamp Duty and Taxation

Gujarat charges a Stamp Duty of 4.9% of the property value. Registration charges stand at 1%. Note that women buyers get an exemption on registration fees.

3

NRI Banking Protocols

NRI buyers must transact through NRE or NRO accounts complying with FEMA regulations. We provide direct legal assistance for NRI capital repatriation paths.

4

Possession Checklists

Conduct detailed plumbing, plastering, wiring, and finishing inspections before signing the final possession handover. Our brokers accompany you during execution.

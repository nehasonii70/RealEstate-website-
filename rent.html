Tenant Legal Guide (Surat, Gujarat)

11-Month Notarized Rental Contract

In Gujarat, standard residential tenancy agreements span 11 months. Contracts above 12 months require mandatory sub-registrar registration and stamp duty payment.

Mandatory Surat Police Verification

Tenancy verification by Surat Police is legally mandatory. The landlord must submit the verified tenant form alongside identity and job documents before move-in.

Society Maintenance & Utility Bills

Determine if society maintenance charges are included in the monthly rent. Under standard Surat terms, municipal taxes are cleared by owners, and electricity is paid by tenants.
